Flagship Academic UI/UX Case Study

Vehicle Repair Assistant (FixGo)

Project Background & User Problem

Motorcycle and scooter riders frequently face sudden mechanical breakdowns, flat tires, and stalls in unfamiliar areas or late at night. 78% of interviewed riders reported fear of unfair pricing, and 64% struggled to explain their exact breakdown coordinates over the phone.

Design Solution

Designed an intuitive 2-tap mobile SOS roadside assistance app in Figma featuring GPS auto-detect, verified mechanic profiles with background checks, live route tracking, and transparent upfront cost calculators for parts and labor.

Usability Testing & Outcomes

Tested prototypes with 12 daily commuters: Reduced average emergency booking time from 2m 14s to 38 seconds, achieved a 92% task completion success rate, and earned a 4.8 / 5.0 user trust and safety rating.
